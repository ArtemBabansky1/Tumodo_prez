How to switch to Vacation mode

Two steps to turn your work account into a personal travel account.
  • Log in to your Tumodo account
  • To switch to Vacation mode, select the palm tree icon on your dashboard

How to add travellers

When you switch to Vacation mode, a new Travellers section appears in the upper right corner.

Go to this section and click Create to add family members or friends to your trip.

Fill in the traveller details

Add the traveller's documents

Confirm your bank card

To confirm your card, an SMS code is sent to the phone number linked to it. Enter the code to complete the check.

A small symbolic amount may be temporarily charged to your card and refunded immediately — there's no need to worry. If the code does not arrive in time, request a new one by clicking Code not arriving?

How to complete your vacation booking

Once all services are booked, they move to the Reserved status — you can confirm or cancel each service before payment.

To complete your booking, open your trip and click Reserve under each service. As soon as your trip is confirmed, a Travel Plan appears in your Tumodo account — a handy overview of all your bookings.
