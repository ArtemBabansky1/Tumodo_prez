# How to switch to Vacation mode
[layout: title-bullets]
[image: photos/dashboard-home-2.png]
> Two steps to turn your work account into a personal travel account.

- Log in to your Tumodo account
- To switch to Vacation mode, select the palm tree icon on your dashboard

---

# How to add travellers
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: Travellers section]
> When you switch to Vacation mode, a new Travellers section appears in the upper right corner.

Go to this section and click Create to add family members or friends to your trip.

---

# Fill in the traveller details
[layout: title-bullets]
[image: photos/edit-panel.png]
> In the pop-up window, fill in the fields marked with a red asterisk.

Please make sure the traveller's name details match the travel document you will use for this trip.

---

# Add the traveller's documents
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: traveller document form]
> To book services, add the traveller's document details by clicking Add.

Enter the details of the travel document required for your trip. If needed, you can add an alternative document in the Other document field.

---

# Optional traveller details
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: traveller contacts and documents form]
> You can also add contact details so tickets and vouchers reach the traveller.

- Email address
- Phone number
- Mile card details

Tickets and accommodation vouchers will be sent to the provided email address.

---

# Saved travellers stay in your account
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: edit traveller profile]
> Details of added travellers are automatically saved in your Tumodo account.

If you want to remove them later, simply enable the Block option.

---

# How to add a bank card
[layout: numbered-cards-3]
[screenshot: add bank card screen]

## Create a trip
Create a trip for your vacation.

## Open payment method
In the pop-up window, open the Payment method field and select Add card.

## Fill in the details
Fill in the required details and confirm data processing by clicking Add card.

---

# Confirm your bank card
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: card confirmation by code]
> To confirm your card, an SMS code is sent to the phone number linked to it. Enter the code to complete the check.

A small symbolic amount may be temporarily charged to your card and refunded immediately — there's no need to worry. If the code does not arrive in time, request a new one by clicking Code not arriving?

---

# How to remove a saved card
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: edit profile with payment cards]
> If you want to remove a bank card after booking your trip, open the edit mode and click the trash icon next to the card.

---

# How to search for flights
[layout: title-bullets]
[image: photos/reports-expense.png]
> Find the right flight for your vacation in a few steps.

- Click the Flights icon
- Enter your destination and travel dates, then select travellers
- If needed, choose a flight class
- Click Search
- Use filters to refine your search
- Save flights to favourites or add them to a selection

---

# How to book flights
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: fare selection and booking screen]
> From choosing a flight to confirming the fare.

- Choose a flight and click on the price to continue
- In the panel on the right, select a fare and review traveller details
- Click Book

---

# How to search for accommodation
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: accommodation search page]
> Everything you need to find the perfect stay.

- Click the Accommodation icon
- Enter your destination and travel dates, then select travellers
- Choose the number of rooms if needed
- Click Search
- Use filters to refine your search
- Save interesting hotels to favourites

---

# How to book accommodation
[layout: benefits-grid]
[screenshot: hotel card with details]
> Click a hotel card and the panel on the right shows everything you need to decide.

- Photos
- Location on the map
- Hotel description
- Room options
- Hotel services
- Important details
- Guest reviews

---

# Confirm your stay
[layout: title-bullets]
[screenshot: room booking and confirmation screen]
> A few final steps to lock in your accommodation.

- Select a room and click Book
- If needed, add a comment for the people travelling with you
- Then confirm the booking

---

# How to search for car transfers
[layout: numbered-cards-3]
[screenshot: transfer search page]

## Open transfers
Click the Transfers icon.

## Set your route
Enter the pick-up and drop-off locations, then select travellers.

## Search
Click Search to see available options.

---

# How to book a car transfer
[layout: numbered-cards-3]
[screenshot: transfer booking screen]

## Choose an option
Select a suitable option from the list and click on the price.

## Fill in the details
In the panel on the right, fill in the required details — a phone number is required for contact.

## Book
Click Book to confirm your transfer.

---

# How to complete your vacation booking
[layout: title-bullets]
[image: photos/trips-cards.png]
> Once all services are booked, they move to the Reserved status — you can confirm or cancel each service before payment.

To complete your booking, open your trip and click Reserve under each service. As soon as your trip is confirmed, a Travel Plan appears in your Tumodo account — a handy overview of all your bookings.
