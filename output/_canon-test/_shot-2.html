What's stopping companies from growing?

As we analysed the market, we saw the same pattern everywhere: companies want to grow, but their travel processes turn corporate trips into a burden.
These four issues appear in almost every organisation.
1
Booking takes too long
A trip that should take 5 minutes to book ends up taking hours — sometimes an entire workday.
  • Traveller data is entered manually, which leads to mistakes
  • No clear instructions for internal systems or external services
  • Support is too slow, so employees have to solve problems on their own
2
No proper process for closing trips
Everything else in the company is automated — except travel.
  • Missing boarding passes and documents
  • Fines and compliance issues
  • No integration with accounting or HRIS
  • Manual advance reports, manual reconciliation, manual everything

How can companies grow if corporate travel stays out of control?

In today's market we saw the same issue again and again: businesses are held back by outdated tools, slow service and fragmented processes.
